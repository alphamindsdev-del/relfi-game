# 🎭 **DELIVERY STYLE (HOW TO SOUND POWERFUL)** 

## **Voice Rules** 🗣 

- Speak slightly slower than normal 

- Pause after key lines 

- Drop your tone at the end (sounds certain) 

## 👁 **Eye Contact** 

- Look at everyone, not one person 

- Don’t look down (kills authority) 

## ✋ **Body Language** 

- Minimal movement 

- One or two hand gestures max 

- Stillness = confidence 

# 🚫 **WHAT TO AVOID (VERY IMPORTANT)** 

## ❌ **Weak Seer Style**

- 👉 This kills influence instantly 

## ❌ **Over-explaining**

- 👉 People stop listening 

## ❌ **Revealing the clue directly**
